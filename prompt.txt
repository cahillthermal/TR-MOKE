Write a python code that can model the non-linear response of a ferromagnetic thin film
transducer in a time-resolved magneto-optic kerr effect experiment.  Assume that the thermal response
of the metal film is a simple exponential decay.  The parameters in the model are the thickness of
the metal film (default 20 nm), the thermal conductance per unit area of the interface between the metal film and
a susbtrate (default 100 MW/m^2-K) that is assumed to have an extremely high thermal conductivity, the volumetric heat capacity
of the metal film (default 3 J/cm^3-K), the Curie temperature of the metal film (default 330 K), and the exponent that describes the
temperature dependence of the magnetization for temperatures below the Curie temperature (default 3/2).  Additional
parameter the modulation frequency of the pump (default 10 MHz), 
the range of time-delays of the probe (default -20 ps to 4 ns), and the base temperature of the sample (default 300 K)
The calculation should compare what happens as the energy per unit area of the pump pulse increases 
and causes the temperature of the metal film to exceed the Curie temperature.  Assume that the metal film
absorbs 30% of the incident energy in the pump and assume that the probe is weak compared to the pump.
The repetition rate of the laser is 80 MHz.  If any of this prompt is unclear, ask for clarification.
